# Changelog

Small improvements made by the improvement loop, newest first.

- Rondebosch fully mapped: tees and greens for holes 2, 3, 4, 13 and 17 traced from satellite (GPS distances on all 18 holes).
- Rondebosch tee boxes with rating & slope (TheGrint): Yellow 72.0/128, White 69.8/124, Red 67.5/119 (men); ladies 78.8/137, 76.4/131, 73.5/123. Courses with built-in tees no longer say rating & slope are missing.
- Rondebosch Golf Club: full 18-hole scorecard (par 72, all stroke indexes); holes 2, 3, 4, 13, 17 added from published scorecards (data/scorecards.json, kept on yearly rebuilds). Setting the pin on a hole with no mapped green saves it as that hole's green.
- Shot marking picks your next club from the distance left (putter on the green) and asks before saving a double-tap duplicate. Recap shows round time. Home shows what to work on next (links to Practice). Add-player button matches the blue theme.
- Scoring Method: round Recap shows SZIR, Down in 3, Both and your biggest leak; ✓/✗ rows on the card. Scoring stats add bogey or better, doubles per 18, bounce back and on-the-green-from-the-zone.
- Share scorecard: a finished round can be shared (WhatsApp etc.) or copied as a text summary.
- Player profile: name, home club, handicap, best/average score and a tour-style headshot made on the phone (face crop, transparent background, studio look). Save as PNG.
- Quick score sheet on the map: tap score and putts, tee shot and scoring zone, then Finish hole.
- Shot tracking: each shot records where it was hit from (tee, fairway, rough, bunker, trouble, green) and penalties (OB, water, lost/unplayable), which update the hole's penalty count.
- Round detail now has Recap (score, slope, rating, differential, front/back/totals card), Replay (shots on the satellite map, hole by hole, with an Edit mode to place shots after the round) and Stats tabs.
- Tap-to-measure on the map shows large distance labels on the line.
- Stats is now a hub with a selector: Overview (strengths & weaknesses vs target handicap), Rounds, Scoring, Driving, Approach, Short game, Putting and Clubs (per-club shot history with outliers left out).
- Rounds moved out of the bottom bar into Stats and the Home screen ("All rounds").
- Other players: add up to 5 players (saved on this phone) when starting a round, enter their score and putts on the score sheet, and see them on the Recap scorecard.
- Game formats: stroke play, Stableford, match play and skins, all using net scores from handicap and stroke index.
- Strokes gained (Stats → Strokes gained): driving, approach, short game and putting per 18 against a tour-level baseline, with a practice priority. Counts only fully tracked holes.
- End round / Delete round: in the round menu (☰) and under the in-progress card on Home.
- Devonvale Golf & Wine Estate added with its scorecard; course database builder now also picks up courses mapped as a single point.
- Home order: profile, handicap, courses near me (search bar plus the 2 closest courses), then rounds.
- Settings moved to a gear button on Home (top right); the fourth tab is now Practice (placeholder showing your current focus areas).
- Readability: map panels always use dark text on white with blue numbers; header and tab bar follow dark mode; stronger label colours. Driving ranges and academies no longer show as nearby courses.
- Golf bag: edit which clubs are in the bag (full catalogue of woods, hybrids, irons, wedges), add each club's model.
- Club pages: True Distance (from better recent strikes, outliers excluded) vs manual distance with per-club and all-clubs toggles, strokes gained per shot, accuracy (on target, left/right, long/short, fairways off the tee, mishits) and a dispersion oval fitted to your shots (typical oval for your handicap until 8 shots).
- Right/left-handed setting (Settings → Player, or Golf bag) sets the slant of the dispersion oval.
- Club picker on the map and in Replay only shows clubs in your bag.
- Club page: club-type icon header with editable model, True Distance from last 10 good shots, adjustable accuracy radius (accurate shots blue, others red), shot history folded away.
- Aim assist on the round map: draggable aim point with distance and plays-like box, automatic club pick for the aim distance (tap to choose another club), the club's dispersion oval drawn at the aim point, and an AIM button that finds the best aim point for your dispersion (on the green, away from water and bunkers).
- Set pin (today's hole position) and set tee per hole; distances run from the tee when your GPS isn't on the hole, so the map also works when previewing at home.
- Track on/off switch for shot tracking, and a 10-step map tour (shown once, replay from the round menu).
- New course page / round setup: satellite photo hero with weather, holes/par/length/rating-slope for the chosen tee, Course preview and Play buttons, fold-out scorecard, and tap-to-pick rows for tee box (Men/Women), round (18 / front 9 / back 9), date, game format, handicap stepper, and players. Option to leave a round out of the handicap.
- Play tab without a course: search, nearby courses and your saved courses.
- Devonvale tee data (Yellow, White, Blue, Red with rating and slope).
- Club distance estimates: set your driver and 7-iron (Quick setup in Golf bag) and every other club is estimated (irons ~7.5% of the 7-iron apart, woods from the driver, hybrids alongside the long irons). Your own manual or tracked distances always win.
- Wedge part swings (80/60/40%) feed club suggestions, e.g. "Pitching wedge 60%".
- Import club distances from a link (open it, or paste it in Golf bag → Import distances).
- Hole-up GPS map: the map turns so the green is at the top and you (or the tee) at the bottom centre, zoomed so the whole hole fits. Labels stay upright; pan, pinch-zoom, double-tap zoom, tap-to-aim and dragging the aim point all work on the turned map. ⌖ re-frames the hole.
- "On this hole" check is now based on the hole's length, so walking to the next tee no longer measures from the previous hole.
- Fix: course page no longer keeps retrying the weather when there's no signal.
- Closest courses: always uses a fresh, accurate GPS fix (re-checked when you come back to the app, every 2 minutes on Home/Play, or tap ↻), includes your saved and hand-entered courses such as Devonvale, removes duplicate entries and shows when the location was last updated.
- GPS map follows you: re-orients and re-frames as your GPS position settles or you walk (fixes the green showing at the bottom after a rough first GPS fix on iPhone). Panning or zooming by hand pauses following; ⌖, changing hole or finishing a hole turns it back on.
- Aim labels no longer overlap: the distance-to-pin label sits further along the line and distance rings near the aim point hide their number.
- Map this course (in Course preview): for any hole without map data, tap the tee, tap the green, and optionally trace the green's edge for front/back distances. Saved on the phone and used by the GPS map. "Send course map" shares the drawing so it can be added to the app for everyone.
- Course preview keeps your zoom/position while you work, and the hole-info box no longer blocks taps.
- Practice tab: drills for putting, short game, approach and off the tee, ordered by your weakest area (strokes gained, or focus areas until you have fully tracked holes). Log a score per session with ± buttons; best, average and history per drill. Included in backups.
- Backup reminder on Home every 3 new rounds (rounds only live on the phone); "Later" snoozes it for a week.
- GPS map: "On hole N's tee? Go to hole N" when you're standing on the next tee after scoring the current hole.
- Course page: short "No rating & slope yet" note with an Add tee button instead of a long message.
- Fix: handicap index number was invisible on the Stats overview.
- "Round paused" message shortened.
